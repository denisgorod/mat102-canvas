---
title: "Put the questions on the edges"
role: beat
---

The methodology is one sentence:

>[!idea]
**Nodes are ideas. Edges are the question that makes the next idea necessary.**

Not "prerequisite." Not "see also." An actual question, in a student's voice, that a student
would ask *at that moment* — and whose answer is the node it points to.

```
What is a definition?
   │
   └─ "Why can't I just use my intuition
       for what a word means?"
   ▼
Definitions vs intuition
```

Two things fall out of this immediately:

- **Motivation stops being a preamble.** It's the edge you travelled to get here.
- **The graph is walkable.** From any node you can ask "what's next?" and get real options,
  not a page number.
