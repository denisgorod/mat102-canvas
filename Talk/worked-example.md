An intro-to-proofs course at UTM, mapped end to end.

- **351 nodes** — 166 ideas, 185 exercises
- **415 edges** — every one labelled with a question
- Authored in Obsidian, served as a static site, no backend

>[!question] What students actually see
They land on a node, read it, and are offered the questions that node *provokes*.
Choosing one is the navigation.

A real chain from the opening of the course — each arrow is an edge label:

**Reading math is different**
↓ *"What are these labeled blocks in a math textbook?"*
**What is a definition?**
↓ *"Once we have definitions, what do we do with them?"*
**Theorems and friends**
↓ *"How reliable are these results?"*
**Results are infallible**
↓ *"How can anything be absolutely certain?"*
**What is a proof?**

Four questions is the whole distance from "textbooks look weird" to "here is what a proof is for."
