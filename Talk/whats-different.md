Fair. Three differences that actually matter:

1. **The edges carry questions, not relation-names.** "Prerequisite" tells a student nothing.
   *"How can anything be absolutely certain?"* tells them why to keep reading.
2. **It's the delivery surface, not a diagram about the course.** Students read *in* it. A concept
   map on slide 2 gets looked at once.
3. **It's maintained, and it's testable.** See the previous node.

And the honest part:

| edge type | count |
|---|---|
| prerequisite | 399 |
| related | 12 |
| analogy | 4 |

>[!idea]
96% of our edges are still a spine. The inquiry framing is real in the *labels* and aspirational in
the *topology*. Cross-links are the hard, unfinished part.
