---
title: "Why does the course feel incoherent?"
role: beat
---

Students who pass every test still say the course felt like a **list of unrelated tricks**.

We usually read that as a study-skills problem. It isn't. It's a *structural* one: we hand
students a sequence and expect them to reconstruct the reasoning that produced it.

>[!question] Ask the room
Think of the last topic you taught. Could a student tell you **why it came after the previous one** —
not "it's on the syllabus," but the actual reason?

The order in our heads is motivated. The order on the page is just an order.
