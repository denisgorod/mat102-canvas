You do **not** need any of our machinery to get the value. The tooling came last, and only because
the map got big.

>[!question] The whole recipe
Take one week of your course. Write the 8–10 ideas as nodes. Then write the **question** that gets
you from each one to the next. Where you can't write an honest question, you've found a gap —
that's the payoff, and it costs an afternoon.

What we'd tell you to add, in order, and only when it hurts not to:

1. Plain markdown files, one per idea — nothing else
2. Version control, once a second person is editing
3. A sync check, once the diagram and the text can disagree
4. Student intake, once students are asking better questions than you're inventing

Steps 3 and 4 are where our repo lives. Step 1 is where the pedagogy lives.
