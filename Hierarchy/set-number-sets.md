>[!d] Definition
> The **integers**, **positive integers**, **non-negative integers** and **rationals** are
> $$\mathbb{Z} = \{\dots, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, \dots\}, \quad \mathbb{Z}^{+} = \{1, 2, 3, \dots\}, \quad \mathbb{Z}^{*} = \{0, 1, 2, \dots\},$$
> $$\mathbb{Q} = \left\{\tfrac{a}{b} : a, b \in \mathbb{Z},\ b \neq 0\right\}.$$
> The **real numbers** $\mathbb{R}$ are, informally, all points of the number line, including irrationals such as $\sqrt{2}$ and $\pi$. The sets are nested, with $\mathbb{Z} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ as $n = \tfrac{n}{1}$:
> $$\mathbb{Z}^{+} \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^{*} \subseteq \mathbb{Z} \subseteq \mathbb{Q} \subseteq \mathbb{R}.$$

>[!info] Note (notation)
> Authors disagree on whether $\mathbb{N}$ contains $0$, so the course writes $\mathbb{Z}^{+}$ or $\mathbb{Z}^{*}$; where $\mathbb{N}$ appears, for cardinality, it is $\{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$. Caution: in group theory a star means nonzero or units ($\mathbb{Q}^{*}$, $\mathbb{Z}_n^{*}$), whereas $\mathbb{Z}^{*}$ here is $\{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$.
