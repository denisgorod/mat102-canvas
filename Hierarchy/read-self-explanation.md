>[!s] Method
> **Self-explanation**: after each line of a proof, before reading on, identify (1) its main idea; (2) how it follows from earlier lines, definitions or known results; (3) any question it raises.

>[!e] Worked example
> Proof that a sum of three even integers is even. "$a = 2k$, $b = 2\ell$, $c = 2m$ with $k, \ell, m \in \mathbb{Z}$": the hypothesis makes $a, b, c$ even, and the definition of even supplies these integers. "$a + b + c = 2(k + \ell + m)$ is even": a sum of integers is an integer, so the definition of even applies.

>[!warning] Common error
> Paraphrasing ("$a$, $b$, $c$ are multiples of $2$") restates without adding a logical connection; monitoring ("OK, I see") only reports reading. Neither is self-explanation, whose test is that the reason for each step can be stated.
